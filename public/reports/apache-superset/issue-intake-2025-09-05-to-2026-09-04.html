Method

What the four metrics mean

Scope

Public issues opened in apache/superset from 5 September 2025 through 4 September 2026. Pull requests are excluded.

Volume and solve rate

Volume is direct GitHub metadata. A solve requires a closed bug plus a PR cross-reference, explicit fix/merge wording, or closes-issue label; it is stricter than closure and may undercount private or implicit fixes.

Latency

The 16.4-day median and 216.2-day p90 use public timestamps from the 726 bugs closed during the cohort snapshot.

Engineering savings

Savings are a scenario model, not measured labor. The base case assumes 20 minutes per intake, 30 minutes per human clarification, 10 hours per demonstrated solve, one retained review hour per PR, and 2,000 hours per FTE.

Usage benchmark

The ≈ $4,104 base estimate applies published GPT-5.1 Medium token rates plus a 10% VM/network allowance. Actual cost depends on included quota or contracted ACU pricing. Sources: plans, usage, and model pricing.
